Contact Offex Audio

Offex Audio is a small, independently run project, and there is one direct way to reach it: email. Whether you have found a bug, want to suggest an improvement, or need to ask about privacy or business matters, a clear message to the address below is the fastest route to a reply.

How to reach us

Email: deepu.yrf@gmail.com

This inbox is monitored by the developer who runs the site. There is no phone line, no live chat, and no support portal — email is the channel, and it is checked as regularly as possible.

Reporting a bug

If a tool is not behaving as it should, a detailed report makes it far easier to reproduce and fix the problem. When you write in, please include:

  • The tool name. For example, "Remove Silence" or "Clean Background Noise".
  • What you expected to happen and what actually happened.
  • The exact error message, copied word for word if one appeared. Screenshots are welcome.
  • Your browser and device. For example, Chrome on Windows 11, Safari on an iPhone, or Firefox on Android.
  • The audio format and rough length of the file you were working with, if relevant (for example, a 10-minute MP3 or a 3-minute WAV).
  • Steps to reproduce it, if the problem happens in a particular order.

What to expect

Replies are handled by one person, so response times vary. Most messages get a response within a few days, though busy periods can take longer. Bug reports and messages about a tool not working are treated as a priority. If you do not hear back straight away, it does not mean your message was ignored — it may simply be queued behind other work. A polite follow-up after a week or so is perfectly fine.

Business and privacy enquiries

For anything beyond general feedback, please use the same email address and add a clear subject line so it can be routed correctly:

  • Business or partnership enquiries: start your subject line with "Business".
  • Privacy or data enquiries, including requests relating to your data: start your subject line with "Privacy".

Being specific in the subject line helps ensure your message is not missed.

A note on what not to send

Please do not send confidential, sensitive, or private audio through email, and do not attach files containing personal, medical, legal, or financial information. Email is not a secure channel for that kind of material, and it is not needed to diagnose a problem — a description of the file, its format, and its length is almost always enough. If a bug only appears with a particular file, describe what is distinctive about it rather than sending the file itself. When in doubt, leave it out.

Thank you for taking the time to write in. Clear, specific reports and suggestions are what keep a small project like this improving.
