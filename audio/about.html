About Us

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Offex Audio is a browser-based audio utility platform created and maintained by Deepu. The site provides practical tools for creators, students, podcasters, and everyday users who need to clean or separate audio without installing desktop software.

Available tools include silence removal, speech and background-noise enhancement, and voice/BGM separation. Processing is performed by a connected audio-processing backend and the resulting files are returned to the browser for playback and download.

Our approach

We aim to keep controls simple, show transparent processing status, and publish useful educational guides about audio editing. Automated processing can produce artifacts, so users should review results before publishing or using them commercially.
