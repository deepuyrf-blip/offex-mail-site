Audio editing guides for cleaner voice recordings

Practical articles about the everyday problems that show up when you record and edit audio: unwanted silence, background noise, hum and hiss, echo and reverb, voice mixed with music, and the format choices that trip people up.

Where to start

If your recording sounds messy, the fastest wins usually come in this order: remove the silence, then clean the steady background noise, then deal with any echo or reverb, and finally get the levels and export settings right. The guides below follow that order, so you can read them as a sequence or jump straight to the problem you have right now.

Every technique here can be tried with the free tools on the Offex Audio home page, which run on our secure server — you upload a file, it is processed, and the result comes back to you.
