Free Online Audio Tools

Silence Remove From Audio is a free online audio utility for creators, students, podcasters and everyday users. You can remove unwanted silent sections, reduce background noise, enhance speech, and separate voice from background music without installing desktop audio software.

Remove Silence From MP3 & WAV

Upload an audio file, choose how much silence to keep, and process the recording. The tool is useful for spoken recordings, podcasts, lectures and voice clips where long pauses need to be shortened.

Enhance Speech Audio

Choose Light, Balanced or Strong noise cleaning. The enhancer uses CPU-based processing and normalization to make supported speech recordings cleaner and easier to listen to.

Isolate Voice & BGM

Use Voice / BGM Isolation when you need a vocal-only track or a background-music track. High Quality and Faster modes use the same CPU-compatible HTDemucs model with different processing settings.

How It Works

  1. Choose the audio tool you need.
  2. Upload your MP3, WAV or another supported audio file.
  3. Select the processing option and start the job.
  4. Watch the live status, elapsed time, progress and estimated remaining time.
  5. Preview and download the processed result directly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the audio tool free?

Yes. The tools on this page are provided as free browser-based utilities. Processing availability can depend on server capacity.

What audio formats can I upload?

The interface accepts common audio formats including MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, FLAC and AAC when supported by the processing backend.

Does it work on Android and mobile browsers?

Yes. The interface is responsive and designed to work in modern mobile and desktop browsers. Processing itself runs on the connected backend.

Are my audio files public?

No public audio gallery is provided. Uploaded and processed files may be retained privately for administration and operations according to the site's configured retention policy.

Why can processing take longer than the ETA?

The ETA is an estimate. File duration, your device's speed and the selected processing mode all affect the real completion time. The live monitor updates as the job progresses.

About Us

Silence Remove From Audio is a browser-based audio utility platform created by Deepu. It provides practical tools for creators, students, podcasters and everyday users who need to clean or separate audio without installing desktop software.

Current tools include silence removal, speech/noise enhancement and voice/BGM separation. Processing is performed by our connected audio-processing backend and results are returned to the browser for playback and download.

We focus on simple controls, transparent processing status and useful results rather than complicated audio-editing workflows.
