Privacy

Privacy Policy

This policy explains, in plain English, what Offex Audio collects, how your audio is handled, and the choices you have. It reflects the current build, in which the core tools run inside your browser.

The short version

Remove Silence and Enhance Audio process your audio entirely on your own device. Your file is not uploaded anywhere, and no copy is stored by us. You can use these tools with the page loaded and your network disconnected.

Audio files

When you use Remove Silence or Enhance Audio, the file you choose is read into your browser, decoded with the Web Audio API, processed in memory, and returned to you as a download. It is never transmitted to a server, and it is discarded when you close or refresh the page.

Voice / BGM Isolation is a server-side feature. If a server processor is connected and you choose to use that tool, the audio you submit is sent to that processor to be separated. When no processor is connected the tool is disabled and no data is sent.

What we collect

  • No accounts. There is no signup, no login and no profile.
  • No audio analytics. We do not log the contents of your recordings.
  • Basic hosting logs. Our hosting provider may keep standard, short-lived technical logs (such as page requests) for security and reliability, as any website host does.
  • Advertising. This site may display advertising through Google AdSense, which can set cookies or use identifiers to serve and measure ads. See Google's own privacy policy for details and controls.

Cookies and local storage

The site itself does not require cookies to work. Advertising partners may set their own cookies. A small amount of local storage may be used to remember a dismissible promo message, which you can clear at any time from your browser settings.

Your choices

Because the core tools are client-side, the simplest privacy control is simply to use them — nothing leaves your device. You can also block advertising cookies through your browser or an extension. If you have a deletion request relating to a server-side feature, email deepu.yrf@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the update will be reflected on this page. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the revised policy.
