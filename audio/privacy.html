Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains, in plain English, what information Offex Audio collects, how your audio files are handled, and the choices you have. It is written to be read, not skimmed past. If anything here is unclear, you are welcome to ask using the contact details at the end.

Offex Audio is a free, browser-based audio utility operated by an individual developer, Deepu. Using the site does not require an account or any signup, so there is no profile of you to build and no login details to protect.

What technical information is collected

Like most websites, Offex Audio relies on standard server logs to stay running and secure. When you visit, the server may record basic technical details such as:

  • The date and time of your request.
  • The pages or tool endpoints you accessed.
  • Your IP address, which is used for security, abuse prevention, and troubleshooting.
  • Basic browser and device information sent automatically with web requests, such as your browser type and operating system.

These logs are used to keep the service available, diagnose faults, and detect misuse such as automated abuse. They are not used to identify you personally, and they are not combined with your audio to create a profile.

How your uploaded audio is handled

This is the part most people care about, so here it is directly.

When you upload a file, it is transmitted to a connected audio-processing backend, where the selected tool does its work. The audio is processed for one purpose only: to generate the output you asked for. Once the result is returned to your browser, the uploaded audio is not kept beyond what is needed to process your request.

Your audio is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing, and it is not used to train models or build advertising audiences. It is handled as working data for the job you requested, not as a lasting asset. You should always keep your original recording on your own device — that copy is yours and is unaffected by anything that happens here.

Cookies and localStorage

Offex Audio uses cookies and browser localStorage for limited, practical purposes. These may be used to:

  • Remember basic preferences or settings so the site behaves consistently between visits.
  • Support the functioning of the tools and the pages you use.
  • Allow third-party advertising and analytics providers (described below) to operate.

You can control or clear cookies and localStorage through your browser settings at any time.

Third-party advertising and analytics

Some pages on Offex Audio may display advertising, and the site may use analytics to understand overall usage. These services are provided by third parties and may set their own cookies or similar technologies to measure ad performance or understand aggregate traffic patterns.

These third-party providers operate under their own privacy policies, and Offex Audio does not control the data they collect. Where required, advertising partners may use cookies to show ads based on prior visits to this or other websites. You can limit personalised advertising through your browser settings and through the opt-out tools offered by the major ad networks. Because these providers can change, the best source of detail about their practices is their own published policies.

Data retention

Technical logs are kept only as long as they are useful for security, troubleshooting, and understanding aggregate usage, after which they are removed or allowed to expire. Uploaded audio is not retained beyond what is necessary to process your request and keep the service working. The guiding principle is simple: keep what is needed to run and secure the site, and let go of the rest.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights over your personal data, including the right to:

  • Ask what personal information is held about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of information that relates to you.
  • Object to certain processing, or ask for it to be restricted.

Because Offex Audio does not require accounts, there is usually very little tied to any individual. Even so, if you want to make a request, email the address below and describe what you are asking for. Requests will be handled in good faith and within a reasonable time.

Children's privacy

Offex Audio is not directed at children, and it is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). The site does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please get in touch so it can be addressed and removed.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in how the site works or in the law. When it changes, the updated version will be published on this page. Significant changes will be reflected clearly rather than buried, and continuing to use the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions, concerns, or data requests about privacy can be sent to deepu.yrf@gmail.com. Please use the subject line "Privacy" so your message is routed correctly. If you are unsure whether something counts as a privacy matter, ask anyway — it is better to check than to guess.
