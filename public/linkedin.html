Is it safe to use a temp mail for LinkedIn?

A temporary inbox is a smart privacy tool: it keeps marketing mail, spam and data-broker lists away from your real address, and it disappears once you are done. For a one-time signup, a trial or a testing account, that is exactly what you want.

But LinkedIn touches money, identity or billing, so treat it differently. Use a permanent, recoverable email for any account you actually fund, book with or rely on — a disposable inbox should never be your only route back into a financial or identity-linked service.
