FAQ

Common sawaal, seedhe jawab.

Kya ye bilkul free hai?
Haan — 100% free. Koi account, koi payment, koi limit ka jhanjhat nahi.
Inbox kitni der chalta hai?
Har inbox kuch der ke liye active rehta hai (server config ke hisaab se, generally 30 minutes). Messages history thodi der aur dikhti rehti hai.
Kya main apna naam choose kar sakta hoon?
Bilkul. Custom local-part likh do (jaise my-test) — agar available hua to wahi mil jayega, warna random chhod do.
Kya isse mera asli email pata chalta hai?
Nahi. Tum sirf ye temporary address share karte ho — tumhara asli inbox bilkul safe rehta hai.
Ye mail asli mein deliver hote hain?
Haan. Ye addresses hamare apne domain pe live hain, isliye OTP aur verification mails sach mein yahan aa jate hain.
