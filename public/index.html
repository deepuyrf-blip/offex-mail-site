FAQ

Straight answers.

Is it really free?
Yes — 100% free. No account, no payment, no limits.
How do I turn off the sound?
Tap the 🔔 Sound button above the inbox to mute it (🔕). Your choice is remembered.
How long does an inbox last?
Each inbox stays active for a while (generally 30 minutes); message history stays a bit longer.
Can I pick my own name?
Sure. Type a custom name (like my-test) — if it is available, you get it.
Can I change the language or theme?
Yes — use the 🌐 language and 🌙/☀️ theme buttons in the top bar.
Is temp mail safe to use?
Yes — Offex Mail is a throwaway address. Use it for signups, OTPs and trials so your real inbox stays clean and spam-free. Never use it for bank or government accounts.
Can I receive OTPs and verification codes?
Absolutely. Emails arrive within seconds — including OTPs, magic links and verification mails. The inbox auto-refreshes and plays a chime on new mail.
Do you read or store my emails?
No. Messages are short-lived and auto-deleted when the inbox expires. We do not sell or share your data.
