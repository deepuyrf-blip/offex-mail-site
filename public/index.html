FAQ

Straight answers.

Is it really free?
Yes — 100% free. No account, no payment, no limits.
How do I turn off the sound?
Tap the 🔔 Sound button above the inbox to mute it (🔕). Your choice is remembered.
How long does an inbox last?
Each inbox stays active for a while (generally 30 minutes); message history stays a bit longer.
Can I pick my own name?
Sure. Type a custom name (like my-test) — if it is available, you get it.
Can I change the language or theme?
Yes — use the 🌐 language and 🌙/☀️ theme buttons in the top bar.
