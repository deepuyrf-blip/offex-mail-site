Privacy Policy — Offex Mail (Android app)

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Offex Mail Android application ("the App") handles your information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. What the App does

Offex Mail provides a temporary (disposable) email inbox. You can create a temporary address, receive emails sent to it (such as OTPs and verification links), and the address expires automatically after a short period.

2. Information we collect

  • Email content: messages delivered to a temporary address you create are stored temporarily so you can read them in the App. They are deleted automatically when the inbox expires, or when you delete the inbox.
  • Inbox addresses you create: stored temporarily, along with the expiry time, to operate the service.
  • Technical data: an IP address may be recorded briefly to apply rate limits and prevent abuse.
  • App settings: preferences such as notification on/off are stored only on your device.

We do not ask for your name, phone number, or your personal email address, and we do not require any signup or password.

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App checks for new mail in the background and shows a notification when mail arrives. You can turn notifications off at any time in the App settings or in your device settings.

4. Advertising

The App may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob (and possibly other advertising partners). These partners may collect and use data such as your advertising identifier, device information, and approximate location to serve and measure ads.

For more information about how Google uses data, see policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

5. Third-party services

The App communicates with our own mail service to create inboxes and fetch messages. Ads are served by our advertising partners as described above. We do not sell your personal information.

6. Data retention

Temporary inboxes and their messages are automatically deleted when the inbox expires. You may also delete an inbox at any time from within the App.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the service, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please do not use temporary inboxes for sensitive or confidential information.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

10. Contact

For any questions about this policy, contact us at support@offexmail.online or visit mytemp-mail.online.
